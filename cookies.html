Cookie Policy

A transparent explanation of how cookies work on our website and what that means for you.

Last updated: April 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, improve performance, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies do not contain personally identifiable information on their own, but they can be used in combination with other information to identify you.

2. Our Current Cookie Usage

The Cuvette Engineering website currently uses no tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any similar third-party tracking tools.

The only cookies that may be set on your device when visiting our site are strictly necessary cookies — small technical cookies required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled as the site would not work without them.

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
__cf_bm Necessary Set by Cloudflare to distinguish between humans and bots, protecting the site from malicious traffic 30 minutes
_cfuvid Necessary Set by Cloudflare to manage rate limiting and server load Session
fs_session Necessary Used by Formspree to manage form submission sessions and prevent duplicate submissions Session

These cookies are set automatically by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and form processor (Formspree). They are required for core website functionality and security. They do not track your browsing behaviour across other websites.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Our website contains links to external platforms including LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), YouTube, and TikTok. If you click on any of these links and visit those platforms, those platforms may set their own cookies on your device in accordance with their own cookie policies. We have no control over those cookies.

Our embedded Google Maps iframe (on the Contact page) may also set cookies from Google. These are subject to Google's own cookie and privacy policies.

4. Cookies We Do NOT Use

To be fully transparent, we confirm that we currently do not use any of the following:

  • Analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Matomo)
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies (e.g. Facebook Pixel, Google Ads)
  • Personalisation or profiling cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Any cookies that track your behaviour across other websites

5. Future Changes to Cookie Usage

If we add analytics tools or any other features that introduce new cookies in the future, we will update this policy and add a cookie consent notice to our website before doing so. The date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent update.

6. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies currently stored on your device
  • Delete all or specific cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Be notified when a cookie is set

Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that disabling necessary cookies may affect how the website functions, particularly form submissions.
