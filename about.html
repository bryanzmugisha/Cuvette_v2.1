Who We Are

A professional engineering firm trusted across Uganda

Cuvette Engineering (U) Ltd was founded and registered in Uganda to meet the growing demand for reliable, high-quality engineering services. We operate across six core service areas, bringing together experienced engineers, project managers, and technical specialists committed to international standards of delivery.

We serve corporate clients, telecom operators, government institutions, and property developers, providing structured project delivery, qualified site supervision, and transparent reporting from scope to final handover.
