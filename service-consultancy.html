Service Overview

Our project management and consultancy division provides independent technical oversight and advisory services for engineering and construction projects. Whether you need a project manager to run your entire project, a site supervisor to quality-check a contractor’s work, or a technical advisor to review scope and costs — we bring structured professionalism to every engagement.

Why Choose Cuvette Engineering

Many clients have great projects but lack the in-house technical capacity to manage them properly. We fill that gap — providing experienced project managers and engineers who take ownership of delivery, maintain accountability, and protect the client’s interests throughout every phase of the project.

Capabilities

Project management services we provide

📋

Project Planning & Scheduling

Comprehensive project plans, work breakdown structures, Gantt charts, resource plans, and risk registers developed before project execution begins.

💰

Bill of Quantities & Cost Estimation

Detailed BOQs and cost estimates for construction, electrical, mechanical, and telecom projects — ensuring accurate budgeting and competitive procurement.

👀

Site Supervision & Quality Control

Dedicated site supervision, quality inspections, non-conformance management, and daily/weekly progress reporting to keep projects on track and on spec.

👥

Contractor Management

Procurement support, contractor selection advisory, performance monitoring, and management of subcontractors on multi-contractor projects.

📄

Technical Reporting & Documentation

Progress reports, completion certificates, as-built documentation, handover records, and all project close-out documentation prepared to professional standard.

🔍

Engineering Consultancy & Advisory

Technical feasibility assessments, specification review, design commentary, and independent engineering advice for clients at any project stage.

Process

How we manage your project

01

Project Initiation

Scope definition, stakeholder alignment, project charter, and assignment of a dedicated project manager from our team.

02

Planning & Resource Mobilisation

Detailed project plan, procurement schedule, contractor briefing, and site mobilisation with all HSE requirements confirmed.

03

Execution & Control

Active site management, progress tracking, issue resolution, change control, and regular client reporting throughout delivery.

04

Close-Out & Handover

Final inspections, defect resolution, as-built records, completion certificate, and formal project close-out with client sign-off.
