Service Overview

Our construction and civil works division handles the full spectrum of building-related works — from structural renovations and complete fit-outs to minor civil support tasks. Every project is executed by a qualified site team under structured supervision, with documented progress reporting and quality checks at every stage.

Why Choose Cuvette Engineering

We bring engineering professionalism to every construction project. Clients choose us because we manage sites well, communicate clearly, maintain HSE standards, and deliver work that meets both technical specifications and client expectations — consistently.

Our Process

How we manage every construction project

01

Site Assessment & Scoping

We visit the site, review existing conditions, understand the client’s objectives, and define the full scope of works required.

02

Quotation & Programme

A detailed bill of quantities, material schedule, and programme of works is submitted for client review and approval before any work begins.

03

Execution & Supervision

Our qualified site team executes works under direct supervision with daily progress updates, quality inspections, and HSE compliance throughout.

04

Inspection & Handover

Final inspection, snagging resolution, as-built documentation, and formal handover with client sign-off and post-completion support.
